TELL ME YOUR NEEDS

Tell me what you are trying to do in China.

You do not need to know the right category. Describe the situation in your own words. I will use it to point you to a useful guide, recommendation, community resource, or local support option.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
  1. Your request comes in.You explain the situation once, in plain English.
  2. The request is organized.It can be categorized by need, timing, and urgency so similar requests are easier to review.
  3. You get a clearer next step.The right answer may be a free guide, a recommendation, community help, or local support.

Submitting is free. If paid support may help, I will confirm the scope and price before anything starts.

YOUR SITUATION

What are you stuck on?

More detail helps. You can describe a practical task, a decision you are trying to make, or something that is difficult to handle because the information is in Chinese or spread across different platforms.

Housing: “I need a short term room near my university and do not know which platforms to trust.”

Healthcare: “I need to see a doctor in Shanghai and want to know which hospital route makes sense.”

Local task: “I need someone to call a storage company and confirm the price and pickup rules.”

Please leave out sensitive details.

Do not submit passport numbers, passwords, bank details, or full medical records. This form is not monitored continuously and should not be used for emergencies.
